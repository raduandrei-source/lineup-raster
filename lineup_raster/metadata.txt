name=LineUp Raster
qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
qgisMaximumVersion=3.99
description=Georeference aerial photos by lining them up over the map by hand, then let feature matching (SIFT/ORB) snap them into place.
about=Line up an aerial photo over the current QGIS map view in a before/after comparison window (masking slider, independent pan/zoom/rotate). Auto-refine starts from your alignment and snaps the photo precisely using SIFT or ORB feature matching, shows the result before saving, and refuses unreliable results. The photo is saved as a north-up GeoTIFF placed by position, rotation and uniform scale only (no deformation). Needs OpenCV (opencv-python-headless) in the QGIS Python - see INSTALLATION.md.
